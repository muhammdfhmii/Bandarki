Bandarku

  1. Build a Road first. Zones must be within 2 tiles of a road.
  2. Paint Homes, Shops and Industry zones. Buildings grow on their own when there is demand (the R / C / I bars at the top).
  3. Build a Wind Turbine (electricity) and a Water Pump (must be next to water).
  4. As your population grows, new buildings unlock: park, school, police, fire station, clinic. Services help buildings level up.
  5. Keep industry away from homes because of pollution. Watch your budget and taxes.
Controls: pick a tool at the bottom, then tap or drag to build. Use two fingers to move and zoom. With the Move tool, drag the map or tap a tile for info.
